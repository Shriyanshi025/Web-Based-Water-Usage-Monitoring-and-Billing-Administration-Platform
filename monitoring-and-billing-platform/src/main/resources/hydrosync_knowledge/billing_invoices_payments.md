# Billing, Tariffs, Invoices & Payments
- Tariff Plans: Customizable tier-based rates with defined slabs (e.g. 0-10 kL, 10-25 kL, >25 kL), fixed charges, maintenance fees, and taxes.
- Invoicing: Community Admins initiate or automate billing cycle runs at month-end. Invoices calculate slab consumption, shared common area water costs, and due dates.
- Payments: Integrated with Razorpay, supporting UPI, Debit/Credit Cards, and Net Banking. Bills update status to PAID immediately upon successful transaction verification.
- Offline & Partial Payments: Community admins can record bank transfers or cash reconciliations.
