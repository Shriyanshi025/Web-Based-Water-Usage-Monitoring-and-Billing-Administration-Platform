# Reports & Analytics
- Community Admin Reports: Monthly consumption trends, block-by-block consumption comparisons, billing collection summaries, outstanding debtors list, and water balance loss audits.
- Main Admin Reports: Platform-wide water consumption trends, multi-community revenue collection rates, community administrator activity reports, and cross-community benchmarking scorecards.
- Resident Reports: Personal monthly water consumption history, comparative peer benchmarking against block averages, and invoice payment receipts.
