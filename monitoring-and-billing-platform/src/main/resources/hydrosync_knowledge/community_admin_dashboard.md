# Community Admin Dashboard & Capabilities
From the Community Admin Dashboard, administrators can:
- Oversee their assigned community's household directory, blocks, and individual units.
- Track registered residents and invite new residents using secure registration invitation links.
- Assign, calibrate, and monitor water meters and their active statuses.
- Log, inspect, and analyze bulk and household water usage logs across all blocks.
- Configure community tariff plans, fixed charges, maintenance fees, and slab tiers.
- Generate billing cycles, dispatch invoices to residents, and monitor payment collections.
- Track outstanding receivables, unpaid bills, and download collection ledgers.
- Resolve resident support tickets and assign them to maintenance personnel.
- Monitor water balance, distribution loss, and identify outlier households exhibiting abnormal water spikes or potential pipe bursts.
- Access reports and analytics: consumption trends, revenue collection rates, and block-by-block distribution.
