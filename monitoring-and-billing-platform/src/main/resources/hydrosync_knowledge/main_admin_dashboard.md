# Main Admin Dashboard & Capabilities
Main Admins are the platform owners with global, cross-community visibility. They can:
- Create, manage, and configure communities across various cities.
- Review, approve, or reject new Community Admin registrations.
- View system-wide telemetry across all registered communities, aggregate bulk water purchases, and platform-wide household consumption.
- Monitor global financial performance: total billed amounts, total payments collected, and total outstanding platform balances.
- Benchmark performance between different communities to identify efficiency leaders and communities suffering from high distribution losses.
- Oversee global system support tickets and platform-level incident logs.
