# HydroSync Platform Overview & Capabilities

HydroSync is an intelligent Water Usage Monitoring & Billing Administration platform that provides automated water metering, transparent billing, anomaly detection, and community analytics.

### Platform Capabilities
- **Water Consumption Monitoring:** Real-time and historical telemetry tracking across households, blocks, and communities.
- **Automated Billing & Invoices:** Tiered tariff structures, cycle generation, instant invoices, and online payments.
- **Proactive Leak & Anomaly Alerts:** Automated detection of abnormal consumption spikes, meter offline status, and water distribution losses.
- **Support & Complaints:** Integrated ticketing system for rapid issue reporting and administrative resolution.
- **Analytics & Water Balance:** Non-Revenue Water (NRW) calculation, benchmarking, and cross-community performance tracking.
- **Intelligent Assistant:** Role-scoped AI chatbot providing immediate access to personal bills, consumption stats, and platform guidance.
