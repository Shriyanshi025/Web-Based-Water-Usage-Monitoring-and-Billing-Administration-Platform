# Resident Dashboard & Functionality
From the Resident Dashboard, residents can:
- View live water telemetry, daily consumption graph, and current water meter reading.
- Access the Bills tab to review monthly invoices, due dates, breakdown of slab rates, and outstanding balances.
- Make instant online payments via Razorpay (UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking).
- Raise, track, and close support tickets for billing disputes, leaks, or water quality issues.
- Monitor active alert notifications for high consumption or suspected household leaks.
- View personalized water efficiency rankings and historical usage trends.
